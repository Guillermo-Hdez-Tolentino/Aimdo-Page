Data Deletion Request

This page explains how users can request the permanent deletion of their personal data from the AimDo app.

How to delete your data

Users have full control over their information and can perform the deletion directly within the application:

  1. Open the AimDo app on your device.
  2. Go to Data management in Settings.
  3. Select the "Delete Account" or "Delete Data" option.
  4. Confirm the action in the pop-up dialog.

Data that will be deleted

Once the request is processed, the following information will be permanently removed from our servers (Supabase):

  • All created tasks and goals.
  • Custom categories and organizational data.
  • User profile information linked via Google go .
  • Any other user-generated content or records.

Data that may be retained

We only retain minimal information (such as transaction IDs for subscriptions) when strictly required for legal compliance, security, or fraud prevention as mandated by Google Play policies.

Retention period

When performed via the app, data deletion is immediate. For email requests, data is removed within a maximum processing period of 48 hours.
